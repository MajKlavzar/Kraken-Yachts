Safe

A Kraken is the safest sailing yacht on the market. An uncompromising approach to design and engineering prioritises strength, making for a yacht that’s fundamentally reassuring. Features like our Zero Keel and Alpha rudder set us apart from the competition when it comes to safety.

Comfortable

What does comfort at sea really feel like? A Kraken is designed and built by sailors who know how to live comfortably offshore. Care and attention to detail make for a yacht with handholds in the right place, a well proportioned galley that works at sea and systems that make your yacht a home from home.

Equipped

A Kraken is delivered ready for adventure. Above deck you’ll find premium hardware as standard, below, quality fixtures and fittings that are built to last. Air conditioning, a watermaker and generator all come as standard; making for a home from home for extended offshore cruising.
